Link your Roblox profile

First sign in to your FIMA account using Google, Discord or your FIMA password. Then link Roblox in your account settings.

  1. Enter your Roblox username.
  2. Copy your unique verification code into your Roblox profile About / Bio.
  3. Return to FIMA and select Verify profile code.

No Roblox password is requested. Profile verification links Roblox to your signed-in FIMA account; it does not sign you in by itself.
