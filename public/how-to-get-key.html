How to access FIMA Macro

Use your FIMA account to manage access, payments and your device. Check your account before launching and use only official downloads.

Choose a plan Open My Products Download app

Quick checklist

1

Sign in to your account. Use the FIMA account associated with your payment or reward.

2

Check your access. Open My Products to see the access recorded for your account.

3

Use the official launcher. Download the official Hub and sign in through the browser when prompted.

4

Need help? If a payment or access is missing, contact support with masked order details.

Step-by-step

Keep your account, payment and device details together. Follow the instructions shown by your current official build; older builds may still show a license prompt.

01

Sign in first

Before checkout, check that you are signed in to the intended FIMA account.

02

Choose the current plan

Open the official Pricing page and review the plan and payment details before paying.

03

Check account access

After checkout, open My Products. If access is missing, contact support rather than making another payment.

04

Launch from Hub

Use the official Hub to open Macro. If an older build requests a license, follow the account instructions or contact support.

Security note: Never share your Roblox password, cookies, Discord token or browser session token. Sign in only on official pages.

If something goes wrong

Access problems may involve your account, payment or active device. Send only masked details to support.

My payment is not reflected

Check My Products and the payment status. Contact support with the payment time and masked order details.

My device changed

Check the device controls available in your account. If replacement is unavailable or blocked, contact support; do not post device identifiers publicly.

Macro asks me to sign in again

Use the latest approved build and reopen Macro through Hub. If the problem repeats, include the exact error in your support request.
